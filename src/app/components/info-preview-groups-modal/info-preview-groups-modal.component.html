Prayer Groups

The Group tab is for signed-in church members. A group is a private prayer list shared only with people you invite—your family, care team, or small group.

Tap + to add a group prayer or create a new group. Use Current, Answered, and Total to filter requests. Group name chips narrow the list to one group.

Invite others with their email from the group’s members screen. They receive one welcome email with a link to open your group in the app.
