New: Prayer Groups
Dear Cross Pointe family,
We're excited to share a new feature in the Cross Pointe Prayer app: Prayer Groups. A group is a private prayer list shared only with the people you invite. Inside a group you can post prayer requests, add updates as things change, let others know you've prayed, mark prayers as answered, and set reminders so no one gets forgotten.
Perfect for families and small groups
Groups were made for the people you already pray with:
• Your family – keep everyone praying for Grandma, the kids, and each other, even across the miles
• Your small group or Bible study – share requests during the week, not just on meeting night
• A ministry team – cover your team, your work, and the people you serve in prayer
Only invited members can see a group’s prayers.
How to set up a group
1Open the Group tab
On the home feed, tap the Group tab (next to Church, Personal, and Memorize).
2Tap + and choose New group
Tap the + button, then choose New group.
3Name your group
Enter a Group name (for example, “Larson Family”), pick a Group color, and tap Create group.
4Invite people
The Manage members window opens automatically. Type email addresses in Invite by email (separate several with commas) and tap Send invites.
Need to invite more people later? Tap the ≡ icon on the group bar and choose Manage members. That menu also has Settings, Rename group, and Delete group.
5Add a prayer
Tap +, then New prayer. Your group is already selected. Fill in Prayer For and Prayer Request Details, then tap Submit Prayer Request.
6Pray together
Your prayer now appears in the group. Members can tap Add Update or Pray For. The ≡ menu on the prayer card lets you Add prayer reminder, Mark as answered, or Edit prayer.
Why not start a group for your family or small group this week? It only takes a minute, and it's a simple way to carry one another's burdens (Galatians 6:2).
